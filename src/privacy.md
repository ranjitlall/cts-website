## What this site collects

This website is a set of static pages. It runs no analytics, sets no cookies,
and contains no tracking scripts, advertising tags, or embedded social media
widgets. Nothing you do here is recorded by us.

Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP addresses, as a
necessary part of delivering the site and protecting it from abuse. We do not
have access to these logs and do not use them.

Pages on this site link to external websites, including Oxford Podcasts, the
Open Science Framework, and University departmental pages. Those sites have
their own privacy policies, which govern what they collect once you follow a
link.

## If you contact us

If you email the address given on this site, we will use your message only to
respond to your enquiry and to any related correspondence. Emails are held on
University of Oxford systems and retained in line with the University's records
retention policy. We will not add you to any mailing list without your consent,
and we will not pass your details to third parties.

## Your rights

The University of Oxford is the data controller for personal data processed by
the Centre. You have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to
have inaccuracies corrected, and in certain circumstances to have data erased or
its processing restricted.

To exercise any of these rights, or to raise a concern, contact the Centre in
the first instance, or the University's Information Compliance Team at
<a href="mailto:data.protection@admin.ox.ac.uk">data.protection@admin.ox.ac.uk</a>.
You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at
<a href="https://ico.org.uk">ico.org.uk</a>.

Further detail is available in the
<a href="https://compliance.admin.ox.ac.uk/data-protection-policy">University of Oxford's data protection policy</a>.
