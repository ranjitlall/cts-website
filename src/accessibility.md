We want this site to be usable by as many people as possible.

The site has been built to meet WCAG 2.1 AA where practicable: text meets
contrast requirements, all functionality works from a keyboard, headings follow
a logical order, images carry alternative text, and animation is suppressed for
visitors whose systems request reduced motion.

If you find something you cannot access, please email us and we will fix it.
